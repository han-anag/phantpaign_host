### Donovan's Questioning
Donovan greeted the party, saying that he had been looking over the newspaper clippings on the walls, along with going through some of Donovan's belongings, and was wondering if they could explain some things he was confused about. The party said sure, and so Donovan opened up by gesturing at the newspaper clippings--including one of an "anonymous former colleague of Donovan's" stating he wasn't a pleasant person--and commented that "this Donovan person did not seem like a nice person." Yet, previously Fanna had said Donovan was a nice person, so Donovan was curious about what Fanna's definition of a nice person was.

Fanna started talking about how Donovan wasn't doing well mentally, and how even the nicest person gets nasty when pushed. They also pointed out how newspapers were sensationalized. Donovan asked whether this meant Donovan was a nice person, and Fanna said that he wasn't a nice person, in the same way no one is just nice, or cool. Amanti chimed in, saying how people were multifaceted, and Donovan was viewed differently depending on how the person saw him. Brooks added that Donovan was very desperate when he was alive, and was pushed by his circumstances to some very extreme points.

Donovan moved on to his next question. He tilted his head quizzically, and said how from what he could recall, most of Fanna's interactions with Donovan were quite hostile, and yet they called Donovan a friend. He was wondering why Fanna said this. Fanna backed up again, admitting that they "were a dick to him", but they did have some nice interactions, like doing some favors for him. They wouldn't say he was a close friend, but he was a friend. Intrigued, Donovan closed his eye and put his head up to his temples, appearing deep in thought, before opening them again. He commented on how interesting it was for Fanna to say this, as from what he could glean from his memories, it seemed that Donovan regarded Fanna more as an "insufferable nuisance." At this, Fanna asked how much of the emotions associated with the memories Donovan had. Donovan said he didn't have the full exact emotions, but could gauge the general vibes and surface level feelings.

Donovan continued, recalling a specific moment that happened after Donovan had collapsed at the steps of the [[Eastern Shengdu Temple]]. When the party checked in on him, Donovan was recluse to talk. Fanna made a comment about Donovan having to choose between "being a scientist or a soldier." Donovan was wondering if Fanna could elaborate on what they meant with that. After some pause, Fanna tried explaining that they had a moral disagreement with what he was doing, but other people are doing much worse. At this, Donovan asked why if Fanna considered him a friend, why they didn't intervene over this moral disagreement. Fanna repeated that they were "a dick back then", and that they've improved since then. When Donovan pointed out how they were willing to cast a spell on Donovan without telling him beforehand, and yet were unwilling to bring Donovan aside and bring up their moral qualms, Fanna said again that they were a dick back then and that they've been trying to improve. Donovan turned to Amanti and Brooks and asked if they thought Fanna had improved. Amanti said yes, and Brooks said that while their actions could still use some work, he believed that Fanna's intentions had improved.

Ruminating over this, Donovan leaned in very close to Fanna, allowing Fanna to see all the buds and leaves growing off of the branch connected to Donovan's face. He asked how he could know Fanna wasn't lying, as they had lied countless times in the past, and surely would lie countless more. Fanna reiterated how they had been trying to improve, and that Donovan would just have to take their word for it. Donovan, bemused, gestured to the newspapers, asking for confirmation that he shouldn't take the newspapers at face value, but he should trust Fanna's words. Amanti pointed out that newspapers are different from a person. Seemingly satisfied, Donovan returned to normal conversational distance from Fanna.

He sat up again with renewed energy, turning to Brooks now. He asked Brooks where Donovan's gun was, as he would like to study it. Brooks admitted that he was not particularly willing to give it to Donovan over concern that he'd use it to hurt himself or hurt others. Donovan brought up the words Fanna had used before about taking one's words for what they were, and that Brooks would just have to believe he would not hurt himself or others.

With some hesitation, Brooks carefully explained that if a situation was created where Donovan could examine the gun safely and with no chance of hurting himself or others, then the party could allow Donovan to examine the gun. This attempted act of persuasion failed. Donovan leaned in a mere inch away from Brooks' face, even closer than he did with Fanna, and directly told him "You're lying." Donovan knew from Brooks' memories that Michael had given Brooks the gun.

Donovan continued with his piercing words, wondering out loud if Brooks had sold the gun in the moments since. After all, Brooks had a history of turning around and selling things, seeing as he had done so with his parents' family heirloom. Brooks replied coldly that he didn't sell useful things. Amused, Donovan exclaimed that meant Brooks was planning to use it.

Amanti cut in for Donovan to stop, and Donovan turned to him, pleading over how his body was weak and fragile as is, and if they couldn't just grant him this one request. Not moved, Amanti sternly told him not to do the "weak little man" act. At this, Donovan grew visibly frustrated, the closet thing to angry the party had seen so far. He ranted about how he'd been treated lately, being forced to hear the same, meaningless phrases from the NPCs checking on him day after day: Asking how he was doing, how he was feeling, if his head felt okay, if he could walk okay, and other aggravating platitudes, all while referring him by a name he did not connect to--"Donovan." No one was giving him clear answers, people were acting cagey around him, and all he wanted was some explanations. He rhetorically asked why he would try to hurt others, as he was clearly outnumbered. 

Brooks still was unmoved, stating once again how he would only give the gun over in specific circumstances. While Donovan persisted, Brooks pointed out how he claimed he was no longer Donovan anymore, so it was no longer his. Donovan countered that he was now inhabiting his body, so he had more claim to the gun than Brooks had. Brooks, firmly unwilling to hand over the gun, said that Donovan would just have to take his word for it that he'd hand over the gun once the circumstances he outlined transpired. Looking at Brooks, Donovan gave a knowing, sly smile in return, amusedly repeating how he'd just have to "take the party's word for it", just as they'd have to take his word for his. He also mused how he thought the original Donovan would be enraged and fight the party at the moment, however he was holding off from doing such a violent act, since he was not Donovan after all.

After much of this back and forth, Amanti finally snapped, yelling at Donovan to "stop with the emotional manipulation." Donovan looked taken aback, saying that he wasn't doing any emotional manipulation, and was just trying to clear up some questions he had. He had been sitting here with these memories the party had, and found them conflicting to what the party had previously told him. Any pretense of friendliness firmly gone by this point, the party coldly commented that perhaps his memories were only surface level. Donovan stopped them, closing his eyes and appearing to be deep in thought, before recounting off multiple key events from the party's lives: The moment when Nadyn was born, Fanna being thrown into the harbor by their coworkers at a previous job, and Brooks running away from home. He objected to the party's claim that they were only surface level, and in fact they seemed quite deep. The party stared stone-faced at him as he did so, not wanting to give him any more words to launch more biting questions off of.

Filling the silence, Donovan began speaking:

> [!quote] "Donovan"
> "Last night, I had such a surreal and fantastical dream...." He gazed off into the distance, smiling wistfully. "I had a dream I was a frail and useless man. Aged 32. Hailing from rural Anshan, and with a great ambition. He worked hard. Studied hard. Devoted himself to his goals. And yet, the entire time, he was a poor, deluded, fool."
> 
> As he spoke, his smile faded, replaced by a hard, twisted frown. "The public turned against him, and instead of seeking understanding, he lashed back. His friends confronted him, and instead of seeking forgiveness, he pushed them away. The very people that pledged themselves to his inventions turned their backs on him, and no one was left to help him. Why would they? He was an insufferable blight on society. His inventions did not improve society, like he promised. They were breeding grounds for bloodshed. Unforgiveable. He brought shame to his family name."
> 
> "If you ask me, I don't think he deserves to come back."
> 
> His last words were dripping with white-hot venom. He practically spat the words as he says them. And then his demeanor snapped back, as if nothing happened.
> 
> "I've realized the truth now: There has been no Donovan. I've been a Spirit the entire time. I was a Spirit that dreamt they were a man, and loved it, but now the dream is over, and the Spirit is awake." He leaned his head back slightly, gazing at the ceiling as he pondered his thoughts.
> 
> "Donovan died at the Taochuan Fair, and I've been possessing this body ever since. How? I'm not sure yet. I suspect it has something to do with that woman's Binding Vow. I do recall a Spirit appearing to her. Perhaps I'm that Spirit? What a curious thought. But a work of genius by that Spirit. A spectacular gambit, indeed. Taking advantage of a timely moment to finally attain a Humanoid form. Who would've thought?"
> 
> He let out a low chuckle. "I feel bad for that woman. She clings to an idea that had been dead far before Donovan's vital signs ceased. But still, I should thank her for what she's done. Her loyalty to the man, the idea, even, is admirable. She has not come out of this unscathed for such devotion. It should be rewarded." He tilted his head and gazed inquisitively at the party. "Don't you think?"

The party did not reply. Continuing, Donovan remarked how he thinks he might be part of the party in some way: After all, he had their memories. He concluded that for whatever reason, he was in this body now, and while figuring out why this was the case, the conversation has been invaluable to his efforts. He smiled warmly at the party, thanking them. As he said these words, the branch coming off his face grew a little taller, the buds and leaves widening and opening slightly.

As the party was leaving, Fanna turned to Donovan, telling him "not to hurt the Plant Constructs." Donovan immediately sprung up, pointing out with amusement "Ah, so you don't trust me." While Brooks and Amanti cringed, Donovan mused how previously they agreed how he'd have to take them for their word, and they'd have to take him for his word, appearing deeply saddened at the revelation that the party did not trust him. Still, he said that he'd check in with the party about the "circumstances" Brooks said, looking forward to the possibility of being able to examine Donovan's gun. The party mumbled confirmation they heard him, before quickly scurrying out, deeply uncomfortable with how Donovan had acted.

The session thus ended with the party commenting that Donovan was delusional and that they needed to tell Michael.
