## Key Learnings
- The weapon Louisa had used on Donovan when she stabbed him was a special Needle weapon. These objects were super powerful magic weapons which relied on primordial Threadworking magic to damage their victims' connection to The Weave directly. Because they were so powerful, requiring constant magical upkeep to retain their magic, and misuse of them could have major consequences, such as the creation of Frayed Threads, they usually were only used by powerful beings like High Elves or other Immortals.
- Subsequently, it appeared Donovan's condition was a result of the Threads of his body to being damaged and starting to unravel, hence his flesh turning black and dying. It's more or less the magical equivalent of his body being hit with radiation poisoning and disintegrating on the cellular level.
- In addition, his soul's flame being cold was likely because of it being muddled and not pure. Instead of his soul being distinctly "Donovan", it was diluted and not wholly him. (See: the metaphor of people's souls being like different beverages, while Donovan's was like Orappilk)
- Finally, Donovan's star in the sky, associated with his destiny in life, had disappeared. This meant that for whatever reason, his existence wasn't being tracked by the Gods anymore.
- The plant constructs, or at least the one that came "alive" during the session, had Spirits inside them.
- Lauran appeared to have placed a curse on Donovan, slowly draining him of his vitality and worsening his condition. The plant constructs' sacrifice was able to stave off some of the damage, but if he wasn't healed in time, he would perish.
- There was a special herb Master Hua needed to heal Donovan, called the Maiden's Bell. The Tigers of Nam had been hoarding them for some time now, and so the only way to obtain one was to go to Nam.
